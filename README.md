Entry for the make.fun bounty **news-image-20261121**: sum up Saturday
21 November 2026's world news in one image.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the bundled front
page, `entry.png` (1024x1024, drawn in-session with Pillow), to
`/output/entry.png`. No network, no randomness — two runs write identical
bytes. It also validates the output: PNG magic, 1024x1024, at most 1 MiB,
and a present IEND chunk.

## The six stories

1. **Ukraine** — Russia fires 400+ drones and 110 missiles overnight; 8
   killed in Kyiv, and missiles land in Poland.
2. **Markets** — AI sell-off drags stocks into correction: S&P 500 −3.7%,
   Nasdaq −5.3%, Nikkei −5.1%.
3. **Peru** — A 6.8-magnitude quake shakes the Andes; Lima residents rush
   outdoors, no casualties reported.
4. **Climate** — COP31 talks in Antalya run past deadline as nearly 200
   nations fail to agree on fossil fuels.
5. **Brexit** — UK and EU sign the biggest post-Brexit deal: food, carbon
   markets and youth mobility (100,000 visas a year each way).
6. **Health** — Pancreatic cancer pill daraxonrasib plus chemotherapy
   leaves tumours undetectable in 1 in 12 patients; 90% stop growing.

Sources: Reuters · AP · BBC · France 24 · CBS News · USGS. Every story
was verified against live reporting on 21 November 2026.
